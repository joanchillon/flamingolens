What it does

Listing at a glance

Price, Best Sellers Rank, rating, number of sellers, fulfillment, weight and size, all in one quiet side panel.

Real profit per unit

Enter your product cost and shipping. FlamingoLens shows Amazon fees, profit, margin, ROI and the highest cost that still breaks even, with VAT handled for European stores.

Saved list and export

Save products as you research, compare them side by side and export everything to CSV for your spreadsheet.

  • Works on Amazon US, Canada, Mexico, UK, Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Sweden, Poland and Belgium.
  • Fast and lightweight. It doesn't slow Amazon down or cover product images.
  • No pop-ups, no ads, no review nags.

How FlamingoLens uses data

FlamingoLens reads the Amazon product page you are viewing to show its data. Your saved products and settings are stored in your own browser.

Sellers can optionally connect their Amazon seller account through Amazon's official Selling Partner API. FlamingoLens then uses Amazon's data only to show exact fee estimates, sales rank and offer information for the products you look at. FlamingoLens does not access buyer names, addresses or other customer information, and never sells or shares your data. Read the full privacy policy.
