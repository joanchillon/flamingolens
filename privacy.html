Privacy policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains what the FlamingoLens Chrome extension and website ("FlamingoLens", "we") collect, why, and what control you have. FlamingoLens is built to collect as little as possible.

1. Data stored only in your browser

FlamingoLens reads the Amazon product page you are viewing (such as title, price, Best Sellers Rank, rating and product details) to display it in the side panel. The products you save, the costs you enter in the profit calculator and your settings are stored in your own browser using Chrome's local storage. This data is not sent to us.

2. Optional Amazon seller account connection

If you choose to connect your Amazon seller account, you authorize FlamingoLens through Amazon's official Selling Partner API login. In that case we store:

  • the authorization token Amazon gives us, encrypted, so FlamingoLens can request data on your behalf;
  • your seller ID and marketplace, to send requests to the right Amazon store;
  • product data returned by Amazon for products you look at or track (such as fee estimates, sales rank, prices and offer counts).

We use this data only to provide FlamingoLens features to you. We do not request or store buyer personal information (such as names, addresses, phone numbers or email addresses).

3. Optional sharing of your own sales numbers

In a future version, you may choose to share your own product sales figures to help improve sales estimates for all users. This will be off by default, only used in combined, anonymous form, and you can turn it off at any time.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We never show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We never send background requests to Amazon on your behalf other than through Amazon's official API after you connect your account.

5. Where data is stored and for how long

Data in your browser stays until you delete it or uninstall the extension. If you connect your Amazon account, server data is stored with our hosting provider and kept while your account is connected. When you disconnect, we delete your authorization token and seller data within 30 days.

6. Your choices

  • Delete saved products at any time from the extension.
  • Disconnect your Amazon account from the extension or from Amazon Seller Central.
  • Email us to ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion.

7. Security

Data sent between FlamingoLens and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and authorization tokens are encrypted at rest.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell users in the extension.

9. Contact

Email flamingolens.app@gmail.com.

FlamingoLens is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.
